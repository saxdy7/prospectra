# Module 07: Unified Design System & Style Tokens

## 1. Overview
The platform features a distinctive visual identity combining the craftsmanship and warmth of **Clay.com** (warm cream canvas, custom rounded display type, 6-color saturated card palette) with the electric high-tech energy of **Smallest.ai** (pulsing 3D Voice Orb, emerald accents, and voice waveforms).

---

## 2. Core Tokens

### 2.1 Surfaces & Canvas
- `--color-canvas`: `#fffaf0` (Signature warm cream floor)
- `--color-surface-soft`: `#faf5e8` (Top nav, subheaders, CTA bands)
- `--color-surface-card`: `#f5f0e0` (Neutral card backgrounds)
- `--color-surface-strong`: `#ebe6d6` (Emphasized dividing bands)
- `--color-surface-dark`: `#0a1a1a` (Dark feature cards)

### 2.2 Saturated 6-Color Card Palette
- `--color-brand-pink`: `#ff4d8b` (Outbound sequencers, high-energy triggers)
- `--color-brand-teal`: `#1a3a3a` (Enterprise featured tiers, ad modals)
- `--color-brand-lavender`: `#b8a4ed` (AI agents & Claygent cards)
- `--color-brand-peach`: `#ffb084` (Data import, CSV/CRM sync)
- `--color-brand-ochre`: `#e8b94a` (Filters, community, signals)
- `--color-brand-mint`: `#a4d4c5` (Lead discovery, success badges)

### 2.3 Voice & Accent Colors
- `--color-voice-amber`: `#f59e0b` (Pulsing 3D Voice Orb primary glow)
- `--color-emerald`: `#10b981` (Voice action buttons & active badges)
- `--color-blue-primary`: `#2563eb` (Data grid actions & upgrade buttons)

### 2.4 Typography Scale
- **Display XL (72px / 500 / -2.5px)**: Hero headlines.
- **Display LG (56px / 500 / -2px)**: Major section titles.
- **Display MD (40px / 500 / -1px)**: Page headers.
- **Title LG (24px / 600 / -0.3px)**: Card headlines.
- **Body MD (16px / 400 / 1.55)**: Running text.
- **Body SM (14px / 400 / 1.55)**: Subtext, table cells.
- **Caption (13px / 500)**: Pills, badges, status indicators.
